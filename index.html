Waterways Grant Finder

A working list of federal, state, and regional grant programs the City of Fort Lauderdale can apply for directly — water quality improvement, canal & Intracoastal Waterway projects, habitat creation/restoration, and boating infrastructure — spanning NOAA, USACE, USFWS, USGS, USDA Forest Service, and FEMA alongside FIND/FDEP/FWC/SFWMD. Programs that don't accept city governments as direct applicants have been left out. Open period by program

Data compiled and cross-checked from public sources in September 2026 (FIND, Florida DEP, FWC, SFWMD, NOAA, USACE, USFWS, USGS, USDA Forest Service/Florida Forest Service, FEMA, NFWF, the Fish & Wildlife Foundation of Florida, and the Community Foundation of Broward). Every program listed has confirmed local-government eligibility to apply directly — programs that only accept 501(c)(3) nonprofits, states, or previously-funded grantees were deliberately left out, as was NOAA's Transformational Habitat Restoration & Coastal Resilience program, whose one-time Bipartisan Infrastructure Law authorization closed its third and final application round in April 2025. Several remaining federal entries (USACE's Continuing Authorities Program, USFWS's Coastal Program, and parts of NFWF's National Coastal Resilience Fund) carry an explicit funding-risk note on their card due to real budget uncertainty in FY2025-FY2027 appropriations — read those notes before committing staff time. Grant cycles, deadlines, and funding levels still change frequently and some dates above are marked as estimates — always verify current-cycle details on the official program page (linked on each card) before beginning an application.
