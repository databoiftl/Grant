Waterways Grant Finder

A working list of federal, state, and regional grant programs the City of Fort Lauderdale can apply for directly — water quality improvement, canal & Intracoastal Waterway projects, habitat creation/restoration, and boating infrastructure — spanning NOAA, USACE, USFWS, USGS, USDA Forest Service, and FEMA alongside FIND/FDEP/FWC/SFWMD. Programs that don't accept city governments as direct applicants have been left out. Data compiled from public sources in September 2026 (FIND, Florida DEP, FWC, SFWMD, NOAA, USACE, USFWS, USGS, USDA Forest Service/Florida Forest Service, FEMA, NFWF, the Fish & Wildlife Foundation of Florida, and the Community Foundation of Broward). Every program listed has confirmed local-government eligibility to apply directly — programs that only accept 501(c)(3) nonprofits, states, or previously-funded grantees (e.g., several private foundations and USFWS's National Coastal Wetlands Conservation Grant, which is state-agency only) were deliberately left out. Grant cycles, deadlines, and funding levels still change frequently and some dates above are marked as estimates — always verify current-cycle details on the official program page (linked on each card) before beginning an application.
