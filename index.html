Data compiled from public sources in September 2026 (FIND, Florida DEP, FWC, SFWMD, NOAA, NFWF, the Fish & Wildlife Foundation of Florida, the Batchelor Foundation, the BoatUS Foundation, and the Community Foundation of Broward). Grant cycles, deadlines, and funding levels change frequently and some dates above are marked as estimates — always verify current-cycle details on the official program page (linked on each card) before beginning an application. Private-foundation entries are flagged where a city government would need a 501(c)(3) nonprofit partner to apply, since most of these foundations do not fund municipal governments directly.
